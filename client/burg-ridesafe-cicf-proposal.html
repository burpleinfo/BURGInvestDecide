Bangalore Reality

Student transport is fragmented, late, and hard to verify.

Many students depend on autos, private vans, and informal carpools. The missing layer is not demand. It is structure, tracking, verification, and accountability.

Unorganized transport

60-70% of students rely on options without consistent checks or operating standards.

Late arrivals

Average delays of 10-25 minutes disrupt attendance, planning, and early classes.

Safety concerns

Driver verification, vehicle fitness, and emergency readiness are often unclear.

No grievance system

Students and parents lack a clear support path when rides fail or standards drop.

The Solution

A verified, tech-enabled transport system for students.

RideSafe combines route discipline, live tracking, operational dashboards, and verified fleet partners through a single digital onboarding flow.

Verified Drivers

KYC, background checks, active documentation, and operating accountability.

Live Tracking

Student and parent access for real-time vehicle movement and delay visibility.

Fixed Routes

Optimized pickup points for BTM, Electronic City, Bannerghatta, and expansion zones.

Dashboards

Admin, driver, parent, and passenger interfaces for daily control and transparency.

Christ University Context

Built for multiple campuses, staggered timings, and high student density.

Students travel from BTM, Yelahanka, Kengeri, Electronic City, Bannerghatta, and beyond. A reputation-sensitive institution needs transport that feels as disciplined as the campus itself.

Campus Complexity

Multiple campuses and different class windows require route planning beyond a single bus schedule.

7 AM-4 PM Timings

Cars and mini-vans support flexible batches where buses alone are not enough.

Institutional Trust

Safety, discipline, and grievance resolution strengthen the student experience.

Operational Plan

Start small, validate fast, scale by route demand.

The rollout keeps risk low while collecting usage data, route efficiency, and parent-student feedback before full expansion.

How It Works

Simple onboarding. Controlled daily operations.

Students register on BURGRENTAL.COM, get mapped to routes, and ride with live operational visibility from pickup to drop.

Register

Student signs up through BURGRENTAL.COM.

Assign Route

Pickup point is mapped from location clustering.

Allocate Vehicle

Driver and vehicle are assigned based on timing.

Track Trip

Live location, delay alerts, and daily logs run in-app.

Resolve Support

Complaints and exceptions are handled inside the system.
